# Civic Listening Tour

`SuzerainStoryMod.CivicListeningTour` is the first isolated decision fragment.

- **Placement:** Sordland, turn 1, step 2.
- **Purpose:** verify that the mod loads, registers persistent variables, and can add a decision through Suzerain Modding Kit.
- **Outcomes:** `SuzerainStoryMod.CivicListeningTour_Approved` or `SuzerainStoryMod.CivicListeningTour_Deferred` becomes true. This starter decision deliberately has no budget or relationship effects.
- **Localization namespace:** `decision.civic_listening_tour.*`.

This is a technical vertical slice, not a claim of in-game verification. Its text is represented in the source and translation catalogs under `localization/`.
